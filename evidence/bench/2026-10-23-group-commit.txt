Week 5 group commit measurements, 2026-10-23
Go 1.26.5, Windows amd64, local persistent filesystem, ordinary temporary WAL files.
Commands run sequentially after the normal and race suites completed.
Journal ns/op is per batch; ns/command is the comparable amortized cost.
Node ns/op is per command, with at least 64 concurrent callers (GOMAXPROCS rounded).
Each node command is a funded IOC order with no match; includes queue, WAL,
apply, snapshots and reply. Journal-only records are encoded place commands.
These are local measurements, not a service SLA or a power-loss durability claim.

Interpretation (medians across the three runs):
- Journal Batch1: 1,217,215 ns/command; Batch64: 20,734 ns/command (~58.7x).
- Real concurrent node Batch1: 1,249,980 ns/command; Batch64: 32,621 ns/command (~38.3x).
- Results apply to this 64-caller IOC workload and local storage. They do not
  measure per-request p95/p99 latency or production exchange capacity.
- Early exploratory 128-iteration runs with eight callers were noisy and did
  not show a node improvement. The recorded comparison above uses more samples,
  three repetitions, 64 callers and the same settings for both batch sizes.
- No artificial batching wait was added to obtain these measurements.
