Week 7 measurement-cost benchmarks, 2026-10-27
Go 1.26.10, Windows 11 amd64, Intel i7-1165G7, machine otherwise idle.
Measured code: commits b3c2d12 (before) and 2ff20fd (after, "perf(telemetry):
resolve labelled metrics once"). Local measurements, not a promise.

1. The clock (internal/clock). Now = QueryPerformanceCounter through syscall;
   TimeNow = the standard library. time.Now is cheaper because it only reads
   shared memory, but on this machine it advances every 578 us, so durations
   below ~0.6 ms read as 0. The engine reads the clock 3-4 times per command.

2. What metrics cost a command end to end (internal/telemetry at 2ff20fd,
   BenchmarkObserverOverhead): one caller, an IOC order into an empty book,
   no journal, engine with no observer vs the Prometheus observer (both read
   the clock). Allocations are identical (17/op: the metrics add none). Time
   per op is dominated by the goroutine handoff to the owner and back and
   varies 4.4-13.6 us between runs, so a difference of ~0.2 us is not
   measurable here.
