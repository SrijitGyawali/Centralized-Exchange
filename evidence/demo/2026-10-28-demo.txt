[2:00] Where the time goes
--------------------------
  A short burst: 400 orders from 16 concurrent clients (IOC buys at 50000: nothing to
  match, so each one reserves USDT and releases it again, a full journaled command).
  HTTP (every request)                   mean    2.284 ms over 403
  engine total                           mean    2.128 ms over 401

  group commit                           mean      7.2 commands per fsync
  Durability dominates: the engine waits on fsync. Under load, group commit shares one
  fsync between queued commands: 3200 orders/s at a 2.4 ms median on this kind of laptop,
  where one fsync per order saturates near 750/s (docs/PERFORMANCE.md).

[2:40] Limits, and the next decision
------------------------------------
  One symbol, one process: a machine loss needs replication, which is not built.
  The latency tail is the disk's rare 100 ms-1 s fsyncs; memory grows ~370 B per command
  because every idempotency key is kept. Next: snapshots and dedupe expiry, then replication.
  Everything else: docs/LIMITATIONS.md.
